# Human & Structured Design Philosophy

This skill defines standards for creating authentic, human-centered, and editorial web experiences that feel tailored, structured, and timeless rather than generic or hyped.

## Core Rules

1. **No Flashy Gradients or Hype Badges**
   - Avoid artificial multi-color gradient buttons, rainbow fills, or aggressive glow effects.
   - Avoid salesy punctuation or hype tags (e.g., no "¡Nuevo!", no "¡OFERTA!", no "PRO!").
   - Use solid, curated tones: deep charcoal, warm ivory, subdued gold (`var(--atria-gold)`), and editorial copper.

2. **Structured & Human Hierarchy**
   - Every section must follow a consistent, dignified structure matching the rest of the site.
   - Tabs and buttons use solid borders, clean padding, and subtle hover states (`bg-white/10`, `border-white/16`).
   - Treat all categories (Parejas, Bodas, XV Años, Cumpleaños) with equal respect and uniform styling.

3. **Quiet Luxury Typography**
   - Rely on font weight, serif italic accents, and deliberate line heights rather than heavy visual decorations.
   - Micro-copy should be calm, articulate, and respectful to the reader.
